Wedding celebration – Paris (02/04/2017)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Sunday was also the occasion for a faqir of the Tariqa to celebrate and make official his marriage in the presence of the Shaykh and his brothers. Songs, joy and very powerful invocations (from the Shaykh himself) marked the evening. May Allah wrap their marriage in joy and serenity.

The entrance of the groom and the bride:

As custom requires, henna is applied to the hands of the newlyweds as well as of the disciples.

For a good thirty minutes the disciples sang praises of the Prophet ﷺ in order to bring baraka to this marriage.
