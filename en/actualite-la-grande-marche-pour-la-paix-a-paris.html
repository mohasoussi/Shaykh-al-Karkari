The Great March for Peace in Paris

This Sunday, 18 October 2026, we had the honour of being invited to take part in the Great March for Peace, held to mark the 40th anniversary of the Assisi Interreligious Meeting, initiated in 1986 by Pope John Paul II.

A powerful and meaningful event, which brought together believers from different traditions, religious representatives, associations and citizens around a common goal: to make peace, dialogue and fraternity a reality lived every day.

From the Great Pagoda of the Bois de Vincennes to the Grand Mosque of Paris, by way of churches, a Ukrainian cathedral, a Protestant temple and a synagogue, this march reminded us of an essential truth: our differences must never be walls that separate us, but bridges that bring us closer.

Our participation in this initiative, to which we were warmly invited, is a true honour for us. It bears witness to the importance of interreligious dialogue, of the meeting of cultures, and of the commitment of those who work for a more fraternal society.

For peace is not simply decreed: it is built through encounter, listening, respect and fraternity.

Many thanks to the organisers for their invitation and for this magnificent initiative, as well as to all the committed people who keep alive the dialogue between religions and cultures.
