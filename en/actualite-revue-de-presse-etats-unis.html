Press Review: Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari’s Journey to the United States

During his stay in the United States, Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari took part in several university and academic gatherings, notably at the University of Chicago and at the annual meeting of the American Academy of Religion (AAR) in Denver.

University of Chicago

On November 19, the Shaykh was invited to the Divinity School of the University of Chicago for a conversation on death and near-death experiences. He presented, in particular, the Sufi understanding of death through the notion of barzakh, as well as the spiritual practice of “voluntary death,” often summed up by the expression “dying before you die.”

During his stay in Chicago, he also took part in several other exchanges, notably on the muraqqaʿa, the cloak made of patches of different colors worn in the Karkariya path, as well as in an interfaith gathering devoted to the Divine Names, Abraham, Mary and Jesus in the Christian and Islamic traditions.

Denver — American Academy of Religion

On November 21, the Shaykh took part in the annual meeting of the American Academy of Religion in Denver, in a roundtable titled “Islam, Psychedelics & Global Muslim Communities.” The discussion focused on psychedelic substances, their effects on the intellect, and the difference between temporary states of consciousness and a lasting spiritual transformation grounded in the remembrance of God, repentance and spiritual discipline.
