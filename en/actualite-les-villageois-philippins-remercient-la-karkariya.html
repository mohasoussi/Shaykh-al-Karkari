Filipino villagers thank the Karkariya

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

In one of our recent articles, we reported that the Filipino Karkari disciples carried out a charitable operation in which a water buffalo was given to the inhabitants of a village in the region of Kakal, a small town in the province of Maguindanao.

Barely a few days later, the impact of this action was felt in this small community. This time it was the Imam of the village mosque, with the help of the Filipino Karkari disciples, who set to work to organise a communal meal that brought together all the inhabitants of the village.

The meal was also an occasion to thank the Karkariya Sufi Order and its Shaykh, Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, and to dedicate a special prayer to him for the good his Filipino disciples were able to bring to the villagers. The guests also did not forget to thank the Mother Zawiya in Morocco and the whole Moroccan people.

There is no doubt that such actions carried out by the disciples of the Moroccan Sufi paths, like the Karkariya Sufi Order, play an important role in developing a spiritual diplomacy with real impact, in which Morocco has always been a leader at the continental and international level, and whose radiance has grown further under the reign of the Commander of the Faithful, His Majesty King Mohammed VI.
