24 April 2019

The Muslim fair (RAMF) 2019

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

The disciples of the Karkariya Sufi Order presented a stand, entitled “Light & Path”, at the annual gathering of the Muslims of France (the Muslim fair) 2019. The aim was to promote a spiritual Islam by passing on teachings about the path and the divine Light. During this fair, which unites all the Muslims of France, meetings were held, friendships were forged, and many prejudices were dismantled.

From Friday 19 April to Monday 22 April, our stand was never left for more than a few minutes without visitors wishing to talk with the brothers and sisters at the stand.

Find all the photos & all the videos at the bottom of the page.

From Thursday, a few disciples made themselves available to prepare the stand for the opening on 19 April, after the Friday sermon and communal prayer.

With the public, the Light & Path stand was a great success. Many personalities of the Muslim community in France visited our stand.

Marwan Muhammad of the CCIF and the faqir Aissam.

Nader Alami of al-Janoubiya TV also dropped by.

Idriss Nguyen in full discussion with Abdelkhabir.

The women disciples also played a great part at the stand.

Some disciples recorded an interview about Ramadan with ÈRE Télévision.

On Monday evening, the disciples packed their bags and the stand was taken down.

The photos speak for themselves: the event was a great success for the disciples and for the association Light & Path. We hope to be present again next year with important new features and an even stronger determination to come and meet you. Thank you all.
