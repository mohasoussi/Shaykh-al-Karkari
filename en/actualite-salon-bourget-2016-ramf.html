19 May 2016

The Le Bourget show 2016 (RAMF)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

By the grace and baraka of our Shaykh, Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari (may Allah sanctify his secret), the disciples of the Karkariya Sufi Brotherhood had the opportunity to hold a stand at the Annual Gathering of the Muslims of France 2016 (RAMF 2016).

Our stand offered, among other things, the books, in Arabic and in French, published by our Tariqa, booklets compiling in particular Qur’anic verses and hadiths concerning the Light and the patched cloak (muraqqa‘a), business cards, CDs of sama‘ (the chanting of Sufi poems), and so on, so that visitors could discover the foundations and philosophy of our Path.

So it was over tea and a few desserts that the fuqara were able to talk with, and answer the questions of, the many Muslims whom Allah – Exalted be He – allowed us to meet.
Their questions were mainly about our patched cloak and about the nature of our Path.

A picture is worth a thousand words, as the saying goes, and we leave you to see it for yourselves.
