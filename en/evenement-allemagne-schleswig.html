The Shaykh's round table in Schleswig (Germany)

On 11 May 2026, Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari took part in a round table in Schleswig, Germany.

The meeting took the form of a circle of exchange around the Shaykh, in a spirit of dialogue and conviviality, and ended with a group photo with the participants.

Photos of the meeting can be found in the Gallery album.
