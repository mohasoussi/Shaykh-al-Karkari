The Shaykh

His noble lineage

Son of the noble sharif Sidi Moulay Tayeb al-Karkari al-Idrissi al-Hassani, the Shaykh descends from the Idrisid line: through Imam ʿAli and Fatima az-Zahra’, it goes back to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
