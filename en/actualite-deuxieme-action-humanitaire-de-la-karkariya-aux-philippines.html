Second humanitarian action by the Karkariya in the Philippines

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

During this Covid-19 pandemic, we have followed with interest the charitable activities of the Karkariya Zawya around the world, and one can only recognise in these actions the universal and spiritual values that revive the human being in the Karkari disciple, making him a positively active individual within his surroundings and environment.

So, after the latest humanitarian actions of the Karkari disciples that we reported in our previous articles, the Filipino Karkaris continued their work by carrying out two humanitarian operations.

After their action in the region of Kakal, a small town in the province of Maguindanao, a short while ago, the Filipino Karkaris successfully completed two further humanitarian actions that brought joy to the beneficiaries.

The first action, in partnership with the association “Arts and Culture of Nusantara”, was the gift of a water buffalo (a carabao) to the inhabitants of the village; a gift of inestimable value, as this domestic animal is known for its formidable capacity to withstand the effort of agricultural work, in particular rice growing.

The second action was to facilitate the marriage of a villager; a wedding that was also an occasion for the inhabitants to express their thanks and gratitude to the Filipino Karkaris.

The beneficiaries also wished to express their gratitude through a letter of thanks addressed to Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, may Allah sanctify his secret, in which they stressed the importance of his spiritual education based on the main values of Islam such as brotherhood and mutual aid.
