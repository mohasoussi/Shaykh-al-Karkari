  • Nun Journal

    Nun is the first magazine of its kind, dedicated to providing a platform for contemporary Sufis to share their insights, reflections, and experiences received from the Most Merciful (al-Raḥmān) through their inspirations and experiences on the path of servanthood (ʿubūdiyya). Guided by Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari.

  • Elkarkari.fr — the Shaykh's lessons

    The weekly lessons of the living spiritual master, Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari, are published here. Every Friday, at the Karkariya zawiya in Nador (Morocco), a spiritual gathering takes place in which the Shaykh and his disciples perform dhikr and read the Qur'an together. At its close, the Shaykh gives his teachings on spiritual initiation through the reading of the Name that designates the divine essence, Allah. These lessons were recorded in audio, translated and transcribed.
