Donation of a tricycle, a symbol of freedom and independence

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

The tragic story of the region of Gamboru and Ngala (Nigeria), which we shared recently, touched many of our disciples, who decided to look for other ways of helping the people of the region. That is how they met a disabled woman who lacked the basic necessities. The disciples treated her case as an emergency and placed it at the top of their priorities. They raised funds to provide her with a tricycle and medium-term food security.

More and more disabled people are receiving wheelchairs and tricycles from Mawlana Shaykh and his disciples around the world. These initiatives make a difference in the lives of people with reduced mobility, giving them a means of getting around and, with it, independence. We wish to thank everyone who contributes to these magnificent gifts. They bring back the smile of those who receive them.
