The Karkariya donates a van to the Fanantenana orphanage (Madagascar)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Mawlana Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari (may Allah sanctify his secret) and his disciples gave a brand-new van this week to the orphans of the Fanantenana orphanage in the village of Antsogobory (Madagascar). The van will serve as a school bus. The initiative aims to spare these young Malagasy the hardship of walking long distances to reach the nearest school. We wish to thank everyone who contributed to this beautiful gift.
