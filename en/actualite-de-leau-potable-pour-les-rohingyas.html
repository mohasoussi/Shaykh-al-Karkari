Drinking water for the Rohingya

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Since the beginning of the last decade, the Rohingya Muslims have been subjected to atrocious persecution and an ongoing genocide, which have caused the death and displacement of millions of people. The extermination of whole communities, the burning of villages and sexual violence have led to disastrous living conditions. Access to food and drinking water has become an enormous challenge for the targeted populations. “Water for All” has already carried out several emergency projects in the country, which have helped restore food security and, above all, hope for thousands of Muslims. Here are a few photos of our latest community well dug in Myanmar (Burma).

“Water for All” is a worldwide community project launched a few years ago by Mawlana Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari (may Allah sanctify his secret). Thanks to the contribution and work of devoted disciples, the project has already given access to drinking water to hundreds of communities in more than twenty countries in Africa and Asia. We wish to thank everyone who contributes to these wonderful human projects.
