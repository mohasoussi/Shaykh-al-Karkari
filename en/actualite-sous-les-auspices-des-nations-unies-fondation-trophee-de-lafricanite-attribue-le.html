Under the auspices of the United Nations, the Africanity Trophy Foundation awards a certificate of honour to Sidi Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

The Africanity Trophy Foundation awarded the Certificate of Honour to Sidi Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari on Wednesday 16 December 2020. “This certificate is proudly awarded by the ‘Foundation, Africanity Trophy’ to Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, at the 1st Africa summit under the auspices of the United Nations, for all the projects of the Karkariya association carried out in Africa,” the President of the Africanity Trophy Foundation, Nasrellah Belkhayat, informs us.

This honour took place on the occasion of the 11th edition of the Africanity Summit, which opened its doors from 14 to 17 December 2020 in Marrakech on the theme “The Financial Autonomy of African Women: A Symbol of Peace and Fraternity”.

The 11th edition of the Africanity Summit will open its doors from 14 to 17 December 2020, in Marrakech (Morocco). Interviewed, Nasrallah Belkhayate, president of the Africanity Trophy Foundation, gave in three points the motivations and objectives of this international meeting.

The aim of the summit was to improve the economic and financial conditions of women, and more broadly of all women on a worldwide scale. The participants, leaders in the political or economic fields, shared their experiences and addressed many subjects relating to the growth of the African economy, the development of new leaderships, access to technology and the establishment of peace in Africa.

Organised in partnership with the organisation of Algerian origin Council Empowerment Ladies International (CELI), this 11th edition of the Africanity Summit was held under the aegis of the United Nations Economic and Social Council as a video conference via the ZOOM digital platform.

And it is within the framework of its concerns and objectives that the actions aimed at improving the living conditions of women and children, in terms of daily needs, education, wells and so on, carried out by the Karkariya Zawiya in several African countries were honoured through the Certificate of Honour awarded to Sidi Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari.

This honour and recognition echo perfectly the kind of education and the values that the Shaykh of the Karkariya Zawya instils in his disciples across the world, for a spiritual journey that elevates the soul and the acts of the human being whatever his race, origin or social condition.

About the Karkariya Zawiya:

The Karkariya Sufi Order, a Path of spiritual education, aims to bring the servant to the station of Ihsan so that he may then realise himself in worship and vision: “That you worship Allah as if you see Him” [Sahih al-Bukhari], in the journey (suluk) and divine knowledge (ma‘rifa) until his whole life is solely for Allah (lillah), the Lord of the Worlds… and that is why following the Qur’an and the sunna at every moment is a sine qua non for the traveller.

About the Africanity Trophy Foundation:

The Foundation encourages interfaith dialogue, economic justice, cultural diplomacy, excellence in leadership, innovation, good governance, the empowerment of women, freedom of expression, the pursuit of economic independence, sustainable development, pan-African patriotism, solidarity among the peoples of the African world, pride in African values through individual or collective success, and cultural authenticity.
