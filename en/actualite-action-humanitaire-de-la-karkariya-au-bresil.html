Humanitarian action by the Karkariya in Brazil

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

During this Covid-19 pandemic, the Karkariya Zawiya, from its seat in the town of al-Araouit, near Nador in Morocco, has not stood idle but has played an important role in the charitable and humanitarian actions carried out by its disciples throughout the world.

During Covid-19, several humanitarian actions were carried out almost everywhere in the world. In Africa, in quite a few countries such as Cameroon, Ghana, Senegal or Togo, the disciples of the Karkariya planted 1,000 fruit trees, built wells and distributed bags of foodstuffs to needy families.

The Asian continent is not left behind, since similar actions have come to life in the Philippines, Nepal and India.

The Karkari disciples of the Caribbean also took an active part in this momentum, notably through the gift of food bags to families in precarious situations in Cuba.

Today, we wish to share with you a humanitarian action carried out in South America by the Karkariya Zawiya.

The representative and disciple of the Karkariya Zawiya in Brazil, Thomaz Almeida, distributed 600 kg of food and foodstuffs to the needy in Ubirajara, a Brazilian municipality in the State of São Paulo.

The families that benefited from this charitable operation wished to thank the Karkariya Zawiya and its disciples for their human and humanitarian actions.

It is worth noting that Islam, in recent years, has not ceased to enter Brazilian homes. This advance of Islam in countries such as Brazil also owes something to the humanitarian and social operations that Muslim organisations can carry out.

And the Karkariya Zawiya is no exception: since the beginning of this pandemic, the many charitable operations carried out by the Karkari disciples throughout the world follow similar operations the Zawiya had carried out in Morocco, within the framework of a national initiative begun with the organisation of a blood donation by the Karkari disciples, up to the distribution of food aid to quite a few needy families in al-Arouit.

This without forgetting the various spiritual vigils organised by its disciples throughout the Kingdom to beseech the Almighty to protect our country from this pandemic and bring it peace and prosperity under the aegis of the Commander of the Faithful, His Majesty King Mohammed VI, may God assist him.
