80 school kits for the orphans of Bagalbari (India)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

On the occasion of the Mawlid an-Nabawi Sharif, celebrating the birth of the Prophet, prayer and peace be upon him, Sidi Shaykh and his disciples gave 80 school kits to the orphans of the village of Bagalbari in Bihar (India).
