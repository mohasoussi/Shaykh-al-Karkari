Meeting in all “fraterni-tea” – 4th edition (10/17)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Toute Fraterni-Thé Meeting
4th edition

Praise be to Allah for this 4th edition of the “Toute Fraterni-Thé” meetings. It was a spiritual moment, a moment of joy, a moment of knowledge, quite simply a moment of fraternity.

The meeting of 28 October 2017 had the honour, as for the previous one, of taking place in the presence of the Shaykh, Sidi Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari (may Allah sanctify his secret), as guest of honour and distinguished host.

The theme of the meeting was “The initiatory journey and the Light of the educating Shaykh”, and it was presented in a new format: the usual talks gave way to a round table around which gathered 5 disciples, each different in their journey and life, together with two moderators in charge of leading the discussions and asking the questions that everyone asks about the Karkariya path, clearly and directly.

The aim of this circle, besides stimulating reflection and discussion around the theme, is to answer the questions people have about the Light, the Shaykh and spiritual education in general.

The second part of the meeting, much longer than the first, was devoted to a question-and-answer session between the audience and the Shaykh.

And finally, as indicated in the programme, the evening closed with a refreshment, conversation and the presentation of the Tariqa’s books… in complete Fraterni-Thé…

Round table

The round table was enriching both for the speakers and for the audience.

Question-and-answer session with the Shaykh

After the round table, the rest of the evening was devoted to a question-and-answer gathering between the participants and the Shaykh.
