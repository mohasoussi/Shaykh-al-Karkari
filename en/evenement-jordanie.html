The Shaykh's meeting with religious officials in Jordan

During his trip to Jordan, Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari met religious officials of the kingdom.

At the end of the meeting, the Shaykh wrote a few words in the guest book of the institution receiving him, as a sign of thanks and fraternity.

Photos of the meeting can be found in the Gallery album.
