Moulay ʿImrane ibn Salem

Eleventh century · Middle Atlas (Tadla)

Moulay ʿImrane ibn Salem is the son of Moulay Salem. According to the genealogical tradition, he emigrated to the Tadla region, in the Middle Atlas, where his descendant Mimoun Abu Wakil would be born.

An emigration to Tadla

The family left the north to settle in the Tadla region. It is there that, later, Mimoun Abu Wakil, founder of the Bani Wakil tribe, would be born.

Descendants

His line continues through Moulay Jaber, Moulay ʿAlal, Moulay ʿAbd al-ʿAziz, Moulay Meʿzouz, Moulay ʿAzouz, Moulay Moussa, Moulay ʿIssa and Moulay Messʿoud.
