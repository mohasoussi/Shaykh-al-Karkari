The Shaykh's visit to Lille (12/2019) – Photos & videos

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Sidi Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, may Allah sanctify his secret, honoured us with a visit to France this December 2019. His first stop was the zawiya of Lille, once again. For several years now he has devoted at least one day there to a gathering open to everyone.

In this article we share all the photos and videos of the event. The evening was in two parts: the question-and-answer session, then the chanting (sama’) and the spiritual dance (hadra).
