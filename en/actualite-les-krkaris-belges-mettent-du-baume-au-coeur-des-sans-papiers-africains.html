Belgian Karkaris comfort undocumented African migrants

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

The momentum of the humanitarian actions undertaken by the disciples of the Karkariya path around the world continues and brings its share of news every day. After several African countries (Senegal, Togo, Ivory Coast…), Asian ones (India, Nepal, Vietnam…) and American ones (Brazil, Cuba, Peru…), it is now Europe’s turn to see Karkari disciples carry out charitable and humanitarian actions.

It was the Belgians who opened the ball for this kind of action on the old continent. The Belgian Karkaris carried out a humanitarian action for undocumented migrants living in Ixelles, one of the 19 municipalities of the Brussels-Capital Region. The Belgian disciples of the Karkariya Sufi Order, with the blessing of Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, visited a Brussels centre housing more than 80 undocumented people from Africa.

During this visit, the Karkari disciples gave food donations to the residents of the centre and, at the same time, deepened their contacts with these people from several African countries. It was also an occasion for the undocumented to get to know the Karkariya Sufi Order at close quarters, and the spiritual foundations that allow its disciples to walk in the divine light under the spiritual guidance of Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, may Allah sanctify his secret.
