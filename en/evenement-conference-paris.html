The Shaykh's lecture in Paris: “The spiritual path in the Karkaria way”

In May 2026, in Paris, Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari gave a lecture on the spiritual path in the Karkaria way. The event was organised by the association Les Compagnons and took place before a packed hall.

Addressing the audience, the Shaykh presented the stages and principles of this path, as it is lived and transmitted within the Karkariya Sufi Order.
