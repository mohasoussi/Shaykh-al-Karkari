Moulay ʿIyyad

Died c. 960 · Ksar el-Kebir · son of Ahmad Belkarti

Moulay ʿIyyad was a local governor in the Ksar el-Kebir region. He died around 960, leaving several children, among them Moulay Salem ibn ʿIyyad.

Local governor

At a time of accelerating decline of Idrisid power, he exercised his authority at the local level, in the Ksar el-Kebir region.

Descendants

Among his children was Moulay Salem ibn ʿIyyad, forefather of Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari.
