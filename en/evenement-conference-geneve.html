The Shaykh's spiritual gathering in Geneva: the stages of the spiritual path

On 23 May 2026, Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari led a spiritual gathering in Geneva, Switzerland, on the stages of the spiritual path.

The Shaykh spoke before the audience gathered for the occasion. This Geneva stop is part of the series of gatherings the Shaykh is leading in Europe, combining spiritual transmission, teaching and dialogue.

Photos of the gathering can be found in the Gallery album.
