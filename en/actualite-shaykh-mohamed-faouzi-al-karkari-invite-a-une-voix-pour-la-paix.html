Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari invited to “A Voice for Peace”

On Tuesday 8 April 2025, the Moroccan Sufi shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari was invited to take part in the first edition of an event that we hope to see happen more often in France: A Voice for Peace.

An event bringing together various figures from different cultural, religious and social backgrounds in order to carry, with one voice, a message of peace, unity and tolerance. Each speaker — religious or artistic figures and committed citizens — contributed to building an inspiring exchange.

Figures such as Gérard Testard, Alain Krief, Nabil Sama, Sandrine Mansoutre, Latifa Ibn Zyaten, Rachida Kaaout, Papa Gims, Udayfa, Laurent Couson, Alilou, Frédéric Zeitoun, Marie Josée Matar and many others took part.

The event took place in the heart of Paris, in the hall “Le 360 Paris”. The audience had the pleasure of enjoying various artistic performances thanks to the many artists who answered the call.
