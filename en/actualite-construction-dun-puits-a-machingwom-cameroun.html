Building a well in Machingwom (Cameroon)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Here are a few photos of the inauguration of our new well dug in Machingwom, in the Noun region (western Cameroon).

Since 2017, the western region of Cameroon and its local populations (notably the administrative units of Bamboutos, Noun and Menoua) have been affected by a rebel insurgency.

“Water for All” is a humanitarian project launched a few years ago by Mawlana Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari (may Allah sanctify his secret). Thanks to the contribution and work of the disciples of the Path, the project has already given access to drinking water to hundreds of marginalised communities in sub-Saharan Africa and Asia. We wish to thank everyone who contributes to this life-giving project.
