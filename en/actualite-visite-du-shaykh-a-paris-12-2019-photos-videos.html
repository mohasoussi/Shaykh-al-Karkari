The Shaykh's visit to Paris (12/2019) – Photos & videos

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

After his visit to Lille, Sidi Shaykh made Paris, the City of Light, the next stage of his tour of France. For his coming, a public event was organised during which people interested in the Karkariya could put their questions to the Shaykh directly.

We also take this article as an opportunity to pay tribute to a disciple, sister Kheira, who returned to her Lord on Saturday 21 December 2019. We offer our condolences and all our support to her loved ones and implore Allah to have mercy on her, to illuminate her grave and to place her in the company of the Prophet, prayer and peace be upon him.

The disciple Abdelkhabir had the honour of receiving the Shaykh at his home. He invoked for him and had his house blessed by a recitation of Surat al-Waqi‘a.

And the following day, it was the disciple Mahdi’s turn to host the Shaykh, accompanied by a few disciples.

In the same hall as the men, but rather in the morning, the women also had their gathering with the Shaykh. Three women unable to travel to Morocco took the bay‘a on the spot.

The translator on the women’s side, who came specially from Lille.

A group photo to immortalise this special moment.

A little group chanting to finish.

Around 4 p.m., everything is ready; we only await the arrival of Shaykh Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, may Allah sanctify his secret.

Maghrib prayer with the Shaykh directly after his entrance into the hall. Find all the photos in the album at the bottom of the page.

Start of the gathering with questions and answers. This time the Shaykh insisted that we not confine ourselves to requests for dream interpretation, but rather develop questions that touch us at the depth of ourselves. Find in the video the French translation of the Shaykh’s talk.

Invocations at the end of the gathering, which you can also find in the video below.

Finally the event was closed by the ‘isha prayer in a group and a farewell full of emotion and smiles.

After the Shaykh’s departure, a snack and conversation 🙂

No, the event is not over yet. There remains the traditional Sufi dance led by all the disciples to the rhythm of chanted poems.
