Karkari disciples bring joy to the inhabitants of Thana Ghora

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Brazil, Senegal, Ivory Coast, the Philippines, Mauritius, Madagascar, India, Belgium, Togo, Nepal… These are a few examples of the countries where the humanitarian actions of the disciples of the Karkariya Sufi Order are multiplying day by day, despite the circumstances of Covid-19.

And this time, it is in the village of Thana Ghora, in Kishangarh Bas Tehsil in the Alwar district of Rajasthan (India), that another action was undertaken by the local disciples. The Indian Karkaris succeeded in building a well in Thana Ghora, a village desperately short of drinking water. The inhabitants who benefit from this well did not hide their joy at seeing such an achievement, given the advantages they will draw from it, especially as the socio-economic conditions of Thana Ghora are not bright. The inhabitants thanked the Karkari disciples and the Karkariya Sufi Order, with special thanks to Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari, may Allah sanctify his secret.

As a reminder, the Karkariya Sufi Order is a purely Moroccan Sufi path. In the framework of these humanitarian actions, it encourages its disciples who undertake them to highlight the importance of social action in the behavioural education of the individual, so that he becomes an element of real added value within the group to which he belongs. The Karkariya Sufi Order thus trains its disciples to serve their countries with uprightness and to adopt civic behaviour with a positive impact on the socio-economic environment of the traveller on the path, whatever his nationality.
