Building a well in Islampur (Bangladesh)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Construction of a well in Islampur, Bangladesh, organised and financed by the Karkariya Sufi Order and its Shaykh, Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Al Karkari (may Allah sanctify his secret).

It is one of the many humanitarian operations that the Karkariya Sufi order carries out regularly almost everywhere in the world.

“Whoever relieves a believer of one of the distresses of this world, Allah will relieve him of one of the distresses of the Day of Resurrection. Whoever eases the need of one who is in want or in debt, Allah will ease things for him in this life and the next. Whoever conceals (the faults of) a Muslim, Allah will conceal his in this world and the next. Allah helps the servant as long as the servant helps his brother […].” (Hadith narrated by Muslim).
