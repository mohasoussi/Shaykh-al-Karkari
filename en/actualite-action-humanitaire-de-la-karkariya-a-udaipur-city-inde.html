Humanitarian action by the Karkariya in Udaipur City (India)

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
و الصلاة و السلام على أشرف المرسلين
و على اله و اصحابه أجمعين

Decidedly, the Covid-19 pandemic in no way slows the dynamism of the disciples of the Karkariya Zawiya, scattered almost everywhere in the world, in their humanitarian and charitable actions for the benefit of the needy.

And as every day brings its share of news, today it is the Karkari disciples of India who have followed in the footsteps of their Senegalese, Nepalese, Filipino, Togolese and Ghanaian brothers by carrying out a humanitarian operation in one of the cities of their vast country.

On Thursday 4 June 2020, the Indian Karkaris distributed bags of foodstuffs to a large number of needy families in Udaipur City, a city in western India, in the state of Rajasthan. Each food parcel contains provisions to feed a family of 5 people for 20 days.

As usual, the beneficiaries of this charitable operation wished to express all their gratitude to Morocco and above all to His Majesty King Mohammed VI, may God assist him.

These Indian families also warmly thanked the Indian disciples of the Karkariya Zawiya. They particularly praised the educational and spiritual principles taught by Sidi Mohamed Faouzi Alkarkari, may Allah sanctify his Secret, notably to be humble, to help one’s neighbours and to see the greatness of the Lord in all creation.

This is neither the first nor the last humanitarian action undertaken by the Karkariya; they will continue, God willing, everywhere in the world.
